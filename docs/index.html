Features

Everything that matters. Nothing that doesn't.

Dosely was built for real GLP-1 routines — from titration schedules to running low on pens the week before your next dose.

💉

Injection tracking & 6-Site Rotation

Log doses in seconds — medication, mg, site and notes. Smart 6-site visual rotation across left/right abdomen, thigh, and upper arm prevents tissue fatigue and soreness.

📈

Medication Levels & Decay Curves

Smooth cubic Bézier elimination curves model your estimated active medication in body across the 7-day half-life cycle so you can anticipate peak effectiveness.

💧

Daily GLP-1 Care: Water & Protein

Prioritize lean protein (+20g/+30g) and hydration (+250mL/+500mL) with 1-tap quick logging to stay energized and manage GI side effects.

⌚

Android Wear OS Companion

Finished on-wrist companion: check your next shot, log doses with site rotation, record weights, and track hydration from your smartwatch with offline queueing.

💎

Dosely+ Ad-Free ($0.99 / mo)

Optional auto-renewing subscription removes all banner ads for just 99¢/month while keeping all health data 100% private and on your device.

🖊️

Pen stock & re-up alerts

A pen is deducted per injection. Low-stock and out-of-stock warnings arrive before you run dry, with refill reminders timed to your interval.

⚖️

Weight journey

Start weight, goal, total change, 7-day average, and Health Connect import — in kilograms or pounds, switchable anytime.

🤖

On-device AI coach

Google's Gemma runs fully offline on your phone. Ask about nausea, nutrition, titration and habits — nothing is ever uploaded to cloud servers.

🌍

59 languages, on device

The whole app translates itself using Google ML Kit — no server round-trips, works offline, remembers your choice.

🔔

Gentle reminders

Dose reminders, refill nudges and a weekly weigh-in prompt — delivered locally on device, never through external servers.

📱

Home-screen widget

Your next dose, pen stock and latest weight at a glance, refreshed automatically.
